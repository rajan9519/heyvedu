Questions, answered

Is HeyVedu really free?

Yes. HeyVedu is free and open source under the GNU GPLv3. There is no HeyVedu account or subscription.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, after the first model downloads. Speech recognition and cleanup run entirely on your Mac.

Does my audio leave my Mac?

No. Recorded audio stays in memory, is never written to disk, and is transcribed on-device.

Does HeyVedu save a dictation history?

No. HeyVedu does not store a dictation history. Debug builds log timing and errors, but not audio, transcripts, or typed keys.

Why does it need Accessibility permission?

Accessibility access lets HeyVedu observe your push-to-talk shortcut and paste the finished text at your current cursor. Your clipboard is restored afterwards.

Which apps does it work in?

Almost anything that accepts pasted text: chat, email, notes, documents, code editors, AI chats and terminals.

Which Macs are supported?

HeyVedu requires an Apple-silicon Mac running macOS 26.4 or later. Dictation is currently English-only.

How do I install it?

Download the disk image, open it, and drag HeyVedu to Applications. The app is signed and notarized by Apple and updates itself. You can also install it with Homebrew or build it from source with Xcode.
